# Roadmap — xlite (Godot iso-tactics game)
**Maturity:** MVP+ (playable). **Stopping point:** a shippable desktop tactics game with enough content + polish; then mobile port, then maintain. Don't turn it into a live-service game.

Structure the campaign as a short linear "chapter" spine (3 acts x ~5 missions, Wildermyth-style) with occasional binary branch points offering a choice of one of two hand-authored missions per branch, giving light replayability without doubling total authored content.
